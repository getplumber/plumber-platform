### New
- When Plumber cannot reach your Git provider, every page now shows a banner saying so, with a Retry, instead of coming up empty as if you had been signed out.
- Settings shows the version of the frontend you are running; it read 0.1.0 whatever the release.

### Fixes
- A page that loads many panels at once no longer asks your Git provider to renew your sign-in several times in parallel; it renews it once.
